Farm Information Dashboard

This dashboard summarizes the irrigation workflow, the sensors deployed across our farm plots, and the key crop growth stages monitored by our AI advisory system.

Irrigation Workflow

  1. Soil moisture sensing at plot level
  2. Data collection and transmission to central server
  3. AI-based data analysis and pattern recognition
  4. Generation of irrigation recommendation
  5. Irrigation scheduling based on crop water requirement
  6. Automated or manual water application
  7. Continuous monitoring and feedback loop

Crop Stages

Germination
Initial sprouting stage requiring consistent soil moisture for seed establishment.
Tillering
Formation of side shoots from the base, requiring moderate and steady irrigation.
Grand Growth
Highest water demand stage with rapid stalk and biomass development.
Maturity
Reduced irrigation stage as the crop prepares for harvesting.
